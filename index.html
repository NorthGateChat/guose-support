浏览、收藏与检索

首页上滑看下一色，下滑回到前一色；轻触画面打开详情，长按制作分享图。在详情中点按收藏按钮，底部的“我的收藏”可查看已收藏颜色；“全部色谱”支持按色系或二十四节气浏览，并可检索。

制作与保存分享图

在详情点按分享按钮，或在首页长按。编辑器可隐藏文字或主元素，拖动、双指缩放主元素，并重置构图。完成后可以保存到照片，也可通过系统分享交给其他应用。

为何需要照片权限？

只有你选择保存图片时，App 才请求向照片库添加图片的权限，不读取已有照片。拒绝后仍可浏览、阅读、检索、收藏和使用系统分享。如需允许保存，可在系统设置中调整国色的照片权限。

离线使用与换设备

核心内容内置，无需注册即可离线浏览、阅读、检索、收藏及制作分享图。打开外部资料或向网络服务分享时可能需要联网。收藏和偏好保存在本机，没有 App 账号或自建云同步；系统备份与迁移取决于你的系统设置。

语言、色值与内容

App 提供简中、繁中与英文界面。英文包含色名释义和短篇导读；诗文原文与展开后的中文评述不承诺全文英译。屏幕色值是数字参考，不是历史颜色的唯一标准；插图是艺术化意象，不是实物证据。

反馈时可以附什么？

可自愿提供设备与系统版本、App 版本、操作步骤及相关色名。截图或录屏请先遮挡私人信息；资料订正可附原典名称、页码或可靠公开链接。请勿发送密码、证件或完整相册。

Browse, save and search

Swipe up on the home screen for the next color, or down for the previous one. Tap to open its details; press and hold to create a share image. Use the favorite button in the details, then “Favorites” at the bottom to view saved colors. “All colors” offers browsing by color family or the 24 Solar Terms, plus search.

Create and save an image

Tap the share button in the details, or press and hold on the home screen. You can hide text or the main artwork, drag and pinch to position and scale the artwork, and reset the composition. Save the result to Photos or send it to another app using the system share sheet.

Why does saving need Photos permission?

Guose asks only to add an image when you choose to save; it does not read your existing photos. Declining does not affect browsing, reading, search, favorites or the system share sheet. You can change Guose’s Photos permission in Settings.

Offline use and a new device

Core content is included in the app. Browsing, reading, search, favorites and image creation work offline without an account. External references and sharing to online services may need a connection. Favorites and preferences stay on your device; Guose has no account-based cloud sync. System backups and migration depend on your device settings.

Languages, color values and sources

The interface supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English. English includes color-name meanings and short introductions; original poetry and expanded Chinese commentary are not fully translated. Screen color values are digital references, not definitive historical standards. Illustrations are artistic interpretations, not evidence of historical objects or materials.

What should I include in a message?

You may include your device and system version, app version, steps to reproduce the issue and the color name. Hide private information in screenshots or recordings. For a source correction, a work title, page number or reliable public link helps. Do not send passwords, identity documents or a full photo library.
